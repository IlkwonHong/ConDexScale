# Research discussion mode

Researcher input: $ARGUMENTS

1. Start by identifying the researcher's proposed intuition and what motivates it.
2. Ask or infer the specific scientific question; don't rush to a full implementation plan.
3. Offer 2-3 meaningful alternatives or extensions, with one strong objection per approach.
4. Before citing or judging novelty, search harness/research/references/index.md and references/surveys/ for papers already found, then search for newer work. Add any new paper to index.md (with verified/unverified status and related IDEA). Separate already-known approaches from unverified novelty; verify specific papers rather than invent citations.
5. Help form hypotheses that could be falsified, including a cheap discriminating experiment.
6. Actively invite the researcher's response before selecting an idea; do not self-approve.
7. At the end of the discussion, produce a compact Research Decision Summary (idea, decisions, unresolved questions, next step). Do not modify source code or launch training. If the researcher agrees, append the reasoning to the idea's discussion.md (or to memory/idea_backlog.md if it is still a candidate) and decisions to memory/decision_log.md.
